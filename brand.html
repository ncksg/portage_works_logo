Logo

Primary Lockup

The logo consists of the mark (rails, traversal line, origin ring, and accent dot), a vertical divider, and the wordmark. Always use the full lockup in primary placements.


Mark Only

The mark may be used standalone without the divider and wordmark — for favicons, app icons, social media avatars, and other contexts where the full lockup won't fit or isn't needed.

Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space around the logo equal to the cap-height of "Portage" in the wordmark. This scales proportionally with the logo at any size. No other elements should intrude into this zone.

Palette × Context

Which palette to use where.

Context Palette Notes
Presentation slides Dark — Charcoal Projects well; reduces eye strain in dimmed rooms
Website Dark — Charcoal Primary site palette; consider light for specific sections
Proposals & documents Light — Cream Better print readability; conserves ink
Business cards Dark — Charcoal Dark stock + light text + rust accent is distinctive
Email signatures Light — Cream Most email clients render on light backgrounds
